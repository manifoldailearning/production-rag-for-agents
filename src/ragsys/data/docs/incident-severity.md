# Incident Severity Levels

Internal. Engineering and support staff.

SEV1 is a full outage of a region or data loss for any customer. SEV1 pages the primary and secondary on-call immediately and requires a status page update within 15 minutes.

SEV2 is a partial outage or a major feature failing for several customers. SEV2 pages the primary on-call.

SEV3 is a minor issue with a workaround, handled during business hours.
