# Cancelling a Subscription

Owners can cancel a subscription from Billing, then Plan, then Cancel subscription.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Pipelines keep running until then. After cancellation, the workspace is retained for 30 days and then deleted, as described in the data retention policy.

If you cancel an annual plan within 30 days of purchase, you may be eligible for a refund under the refund policy.
