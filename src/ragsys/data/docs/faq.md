# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. New workspaces get a 14-day free trial of the Business plan. No payment card is needed to start.

## Which payment methods are accepted?

Monthly plans are paid by card. Annual Business and Enterprise plans can also be paid by invoice.

## Can I change plans?

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

## Is there a limit on users?

No. Every plan includes unlimited users.
