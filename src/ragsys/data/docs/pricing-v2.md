# Plans and Pricing (2025)

Larkspur Cloud offers four plans, billed monthly or annually.

| Plan | Price | Pipelines | Rows per month | Log retention | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $39 per month | 3 | 5 million | 7 days | Community forum |
| Team | $249 per month | 20 | 50 million | 14 days | Email, next business day |
| Business | $990 per month | 75 | 500 million | 60 days | 8-hour response |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | Custom | 365 days | 1-hour response, 24/7 |

Single sign-on is available on the Business and Enterprise plans only.
