# SCIM User Provisioning

SCIM 2.0 provisioning lets your identity provider create, update and remove Larkspur Cloud members automatically. SCIM is available on the Enterprise plan from release 4.6.

To turn it on, open Settings, then Security, then SCIM, and generate a SCIM token. Enter the token and the SCIM base URL in your identity provider. Members removed in the identity provider lose access within 10 minutes.
