# Uptime SLA and Service Credits

Larkspur Cloud commits to monthly uptime of 99.5 percent on Team, 99.9 percent on Business and 99.95 percent on Enterprise. The Starter plan has no uptime commitment.

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment for your plan, you are eligible for a service credit of 10 percent of that month's fee. If uptime falls below 99.0 percent, the credit is 25 percent.

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month by opening a billing ticket. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid out as cash.

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance does not count as downtime.
