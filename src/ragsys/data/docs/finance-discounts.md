# Discount Approval Matrix

Internal. Sales team only.

Account executives can approve discounts of up to 15 percent on annual contracts. Discounts from 15 to 30 percent need approval from the finance director. Discounts above 30 percent need approval from the CFO.

Discounts are never offered on monthly plans.
