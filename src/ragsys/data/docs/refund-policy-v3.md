# Refund Policy

Annual subscriptions can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. The refund is prorated for the unused part of the term. Monthly subscriptions are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10 business days. To request a refund, open a billing ticket in the support portal and include your workspace ID.

Overage charges and add-ons such as SSO for Team are refundable only if the underlying annual subscription is refunded.
