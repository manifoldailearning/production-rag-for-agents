# Plans and Pricing

Larkspur Cloud offers four plans. Prices are per workspace and billed monthly or annually. Annual billing is discounted by 15 percent. All plans include unlimited users.

| Plan | Price | Pipelines | Rows per month | Log retention | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $49 per month | 5 | 10 million | 7 days | Community forum |
| Team | $299 per month | 25 | 100 million | 30 days | Email, next business day |
| Business | $1,200 per month | 100 | 1 billion | 90 days | 4-hour response |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | Custom | 365 days | 1-hour response, 24/7 |

## Single sign-on

Single sign-on (SSO) is included in the Business and Enterprise plans. Team workspaces can add SSO for $99 per month. SSO is not available on the Starter plan.

## Overages

If a workspace exceeds its monthly row allowance, pipelines keep running and the overage is billed at $8 per additional 10 million rows. Starter workspaces are paused at the limit instead of billed.
