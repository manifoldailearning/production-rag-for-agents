# On-Call Compensation

Internal. Engineering staff only.

Engineers on the primary on-call rotation receive a stipend of $400 for each week on call. Secondary on-call receives $150 per week. Incidents handled between 22:00 and 07:00 local time earn one day of time off in lieu, up to two days per month.
