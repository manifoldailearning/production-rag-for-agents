# Contacting Support

Starter workspaces get help through the community forum. Team, Business and Enterprise workspaces can open tickets in the support portal.

Response targets are next business day on Team, 4 hours on Business and 1 hour around the clock on Enterprise.

Enterprise customers also have a named technical account manager.

Include your workspace ID and the error code, if any, in every ticket.
